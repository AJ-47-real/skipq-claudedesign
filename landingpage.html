The problem

The checkout line hasn't changed in fifty years.

Every existing queue system still asks the customer to do something first — press a button, scan a kiosk, take a paper ticket. SkipQ removes the line itself, not just the feeling of it.

Small baskets get stuck behind big ones

A shopper with five items waits behind a shopper with fifty. There's no fast lane built for how people actually shop.

Waiting means standing still

A full trolley means a real cashier — and a fixed line with nothing to do until it's your turn.

Promotions are generic

Flyers and push notifications blast every customer the same offer, whether or not it's ever something they buy.
